# Release v0.4.0 and the pre-release audit (2026-11-13)

Context: the author invoked the release skill with `0.4.0` in the session that had just landed
ticket 0034 (PR #63). Main was 23 commits past `v0.3.0`.

Events:
- The skill's three read-only audits ran for the first time in this repository (v0.3.0 skipped
  them): security (Sonnet), first-use dry run (Haiku), doc/test coherence (Sonnet). They
  returned 0 HIGH, 4 MEDIUM and about 9 LOW findings. The seats returned their findings to the
  session and filed no ticket.
- Security MEDIUM, reproduced: the collector of PR #63 walked the whole open-element stack at
  every text cut and end tag. 20 KB of nested `<div>` took 0.3 s, 40 KB 1.4 s, 80 KB 5.1 s, and
  `_http` accepts bodies up to 200 MB. Fixed in the release request (708192d): per-tag counts,
  an end tag pops only what it closes, text goes to open anchors only, and anchors nested
  inside more than 16 others are listed with empty text. 800 KB then took 0.8 s.
- gaze on c7e323f returned REVIEWED and reported a second quadratic shape the fix had left:
  text with bare `<` characters was appended cut by cut to a growing string (1.6 MB: 17 s).
  The work-count test added with the first fix could not see it. Fixed in 211a5d7 with a list
  joined at the flush and a timed test; a second, narrower gaze returned REVIEWED on 211a5d7.
- Both fixes were compared with the parser they replaced on random fragments (60,000 by the
  session, 40,000 and 20,000 by the two gaze runs, as str and bytes): no difference at or below
  16 nested anchors.
- PR #65 (head 211a5d7, merge commit 8e3cc69) carried the two fixes and the bump of
  `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock` to 0.4.0. verify-gate APPROVED at round 1.
- Tag `v0.4.0` (annotated, unsigned, tag object f5bb9b7) was created on 8e3cc69 and pushed.
  `uvx --from 'doifetch @ git+https://github.com/MinhHaDuong/DOIfetch@v0.4.0' doifetch --version`
  printed `doifetch 0.4.0`, and the same with `doifetch[shadow]`.
- The README holds no versioned download URL, so the skill's URL rewrite step had nothing to
  act on. No blog-post ticket was filed; earlier releases of this repository have none either.

Audit findings left as they are, none filed as a ticket:
- `doifetch --data-dir <missing directory>` prints "No records to process" and exits 0; the
  README's exit-code section covers a batch with nothing to do.
- The `ZOTERO_DATA_DIR` and `profiles.ini` branches of the Zotero database lookup
  (`zotero.py:46,56,64`) have no positive test.
- `scihub.extract_pdf_url` tests `startswith("http")`; `_http` rejects any scheme other than
  http and https afterwards.
- The two shadow sources follow any absolute http(s) URL a page names, internal hosts included;
  only a body that passes the PDF check is saved.
- Key files under `~/.config/keys/` are read without a check of their mode.
- The library `fetch()` does not validate a DOI before querying the sources; the CLI does.
- One unclosed-attribute shape (`<a href=x ` repeated) shows a step in parse time between
  300 KB and 600 KB, identical on a bare `HTMLParser`. Cause not established.

Decisions already made:
- The author first asked for a harness ticket on the wrapper simplification, then withdrew it:
  the harness session doing that work was told directly, by session message, the tag name and
  the commit it resolves to.
- The empty `shadow` extra goes at the next release.
